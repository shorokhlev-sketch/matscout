Ask a materials question

Type a natural-language description of a material you need. The agent will translate it to property filters, query the database, self-correct if it gets zero or too many hits, and synthesize a ranked shortlist with trade-offs.

Examples

  • Find a stable semiconductor with band gap near 1.5 eV for a solar cell. Three candidates with formula, band gap and density.
  • Thermoelectric with low density (under 5 g/cm³) — three candidates and compare them. Explain the trade-offs.
  • Suggest sodium-based electrode candidates and sort them by stability. Top five only.
  • Is mp-149 stable? Give a one-sentence verdict.
  • Compare Si (mp-149) and diamond (mp-66) on band gap, density and stability.
